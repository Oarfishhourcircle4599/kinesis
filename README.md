## 🧠 What is kinesis?

kinesis is a bridge between your Meta Neural Band and your macOS computer. It takes the electrical signals from your arm muscles and turns them into mouse movements, clicks, and keyboard shortcuts. Think of it as a remote control that you wear on your wrist - no physical device needed.

Imagine moving your cursor simply by flexing your fingers, or clicking by tapping your thumb. That's what kinesis makes possible. It's highly experimental, which means you're part of an exciting frontier where wearable technology meets everyday computing.

| Action | What Happens |

| Click | A quick tap gesture performs a left-click |

### Step 2: Set Up Your Neural Band

1. Charge your Meta Neural Band fully (about 2 hours)
2. Put it on your non-dominant arm, with the sensors touching your skin
3. The band should sit about 2-3 inches below your elbow
4. Make sure it's snug but not tight - you should be able to slide a finger underneath

### Step 3: Connect the Band

1. Turn on your neural band
2. Open your Mac's Bluetooth settings
3. Look for "NeuralBand" in the device list
4. Click "Connect" and wait for the confirmation sound

### Step 4: Open kinesis

Once you've downloaded the application:

1. Find the downloaded file in your Downloads folder
2. Double-click it to open
3. If macOS asks for permission, go to **System Preferences > Security & Privacy** and click "Open Anyway"
4. You'll see the kinesis icon in your menu bar (top right corner)

### Step 5: Calibrate

The first time you open kinesis, it will ask you to calibrate:

1. A window will appear with a green circle
2. Keep your arm relaxed for 10 seconds
3. Then follow the on-screen instructions: make a fist, release, point your index finger, release
4. You're now ready to use kinesis

## 🎮 Using kinesis

### Basic Gestures

- **Move Cursor:** Gently flex your fingers together
- **Left Click:** Tap your thumb against your index finger
- **Right Click:** Hold the thumb-index tap for one second
- **Scroll:** Rotate your wrist inward or outward
- **Drag and Drop:** Pinch (thumb + index + middle finger) and move

### Adjusting Sensitivity

If the cursor moves too fast or too slow:

1. Click the kinesis icon in your menu bar
2. Select "Preferences"
3. Use the slider under "Cursor Speed"
4. Lower numbers = slower, higher numbers = faster

## 🛠️ Customizing Gestures

Want to create your own gestures?

1. Open kinesis Preferences
2. Click "Gesture Mapping"
3. Click the "New Gesture" button
4. Perform the physical movement you want to assign
5. Type in the keyboard shortcut or action you want to trigger
6. Click "Save"

Popular custom gestures include:
- Two-finger tap = "Copy"
- Three-finger tap = "Paste"
- Pinky raise = "Switch to Next Window"

## ⚙️ Troubleshooting

### The Band Isn't Connecting

- Make sure your neural band has at least 30% battery
- Turn Bluetooth off and on again on your Mac
- Place the band back on your arm - it may have shifted during wear

### Cursor Is Jumpy or Not Moving

- Recalibrate (click kinesis icon > "Recalibrate")
- Remove the band and put it back on
- Relax your arm completely for 5 seconds, then try again

### kinesis Doesn't Open

- Right-click on the downloaded file
- Select "Open"
- If you see a security warning, go to **System Preferences > Privacy & Security** and click "Open Anyway"

### Gestures Feel Unresponsive

- Increase sensitivity in Preferences
- Make sure the sensors are touching your skin (not your sleeve)
- Try repositioning the band slightly higher or lower on your arm

## 💡 Tips and Tricks

- **Start Simple:** Master moving the cursor before learning clicks
- **Use Short Sessions:** Your muscles need time to adapt to new movements
- **Keep Skin Dry:** Moisture can interfere with sensor readings
- **Find Your Sweet Spot:** Slight band rotations often improve accuracy
- **Take Breaks:** Give your arm a rest every 30 minutes

## 🔒 Privacy and Security

kinesis processes all data locally on your Mac. Nothing is sent to the internet. The neural band only transmits raw muscle signals - not your thoughts or personal data.

When you're not using kinesis, quit the app by clicking the menu bar icon and selecting "Quit kinesis." This ensures no signals are being processed.
